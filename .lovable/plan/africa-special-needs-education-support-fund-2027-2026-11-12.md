# Africa Special Needs Education Support Fund 2027

## Goal
Create matching programme and participation pages on NESA-Africa and EduAid-Africa, retaining each site's existing identity. Present one potential approved beneficiary school per African programme region, up to eight schools across 54 countries. School-support participation remains separate from NESA-Africa 2026 award nominations and judging.

## Mandatory security hold
The repository incident remains uncleared. This plan does not authorise running application code, installation, testing, website edits, deployment or publishing. Complete approved containment, independent security review, repository-owner checks and full backup/rollback evidence first. Written **“Phase 0 approved”** is required before the next phase; plan approval alone is not security clearance. Stop after each subsequent phase for its written approval.

## Phase 1 — Programme pages, after security clearance
- Add `/support-a-special-needs-school` and `/support-a-special-needs-school/participate` on each authorised website project. Confirm access to the EduAid-Africa project before promising delivery there; do not change domains.
- Use the exact programme name, joint presenters, foundation and supporter-network identities, principal message and campaign lines from the upload.
- Present the ten-step process, eight eligible service groups, intervention needs, full 2027 timetable and conditional-funding notices.
- Explain regional selection, final due diligence, documented next-school assessment and the distinction between nomination, eligibility, selection and confirmed support.
- Add the requested primary and sponsorship/partnership actions through existing shared navigation and school-support controls. Keep voting inactive with the supplied September 2027 notice.
- Preserve existing colours, typography and working page designs. Use only authorised consent-cleared imagery; do not generate images implying real beneficiary schools or publish unapproved partner logos.
- Show missing official URLs as **CONTENT REQUIRED**; no invented forms, amounts, regional mappings, beneficiaries or statistics.
- Stop with 390px and 1440px before/after screenshots for written phase review.

## Phase 2 — Authorised participation and approved profiles
- Connect the supplied authorised Google Forms through branded participation entry points. The first question is “How would you like to participate?” with all eight requested options and conditional sections.
- Verify that each real submission records its originating website, not merely a label shown beside a form. Voting choices must not bypass the inactive-voting gate.
- Allow multiple school service categories with one principal category; collect intervention needs privately. Apply regional classification only after written mapping approval.
- Publish school profiles only after verification, explicit publication approval, consent and safeguarding checks. Do not expose children's identities, diagnoses, evidence documents or personal contacts.
- Separate interest, pledges, payment instructions, received payments, reconciliation, receipts, allocation and utilisation reports. An interest form must not collect credentials or claim receipt of payment.
- Publish approved-only accountability information and its last-updated date; missing figures are not zero.
- Confirm partners only after approved written agreements or verified contributions. Display sponsorship neutrality and donation-reconciliation notices.
- Verify actual nomination and supporter flows on both sites and stop for written phase approval.

## Phase 3 — Controlled school-only voting
- Require published opening/closing times, approved regional eligibility, recount/dispute rules and recorded technical/governance approval before activation.
- Implement verified email or telephone participation, one controlled voting entitlement per verified participant, anti-bot protection, duplicate detection, regional controls and audit records.
- Permit only verified and approved eligible schools. Donations or sponsorship never purchase votes or change entitlement; no NESA 2026 award voting.
- Keep voting disabled until all prerequisite checks pass, even if September 2027 has arrived.
- Test duplicate submissions, regional eligibility, unapproved-school rejection, inactive-window rejection and voting/payment independence. Final due diligence remains mandatory after results.
- Stop for written phase approval; do not publish.

## Phase 4 — Acceptance evidence
Check desktop/mobile layouts, keyboard and screen-reader access, privacy, authorised forms with recorded source, approved-only profiles and payment-state separation. Exercise real authorised flows on both sites; identify any unavailable flow honestly. Supply review evidence and require written staging approval before any separately authorised deployment.
