## D. Operational checklist before flipping a form to `Active`

1. Create the Google Form in the listed Gmail inbox using the field schema (§11 for award categories, §15 for RMSA regions).
2. Title the form per the standard in §17.
3. Link a response Google Sheet titled per §18 with tabs: Raw Form Responses, Cleaned Data, Duplicate Review, Evidence Review, Contact Log, Approved Export, Website Sync Ready.
4. Copy the **public URL** (`/viewform`) and **embed URL** (`/viewform?embedded=true`) into the matching entry in `src/config/nomination/awardCategoryForms.ts` or `src/config/nomination/rmsaRegionalForms.ts`.
5. Flip `status` from `Link Pending` to `Active` and update `lastUpdated`.
6. Run the QA checklist in §24 of the brief.
