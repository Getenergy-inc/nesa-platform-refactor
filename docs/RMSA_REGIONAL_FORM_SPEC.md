## 0. Public positioning (form description)

NESA-Africa 2026/2027 invites the public to nominate special needs schools for possible
support through the **EduAid-Africa Special Needs Schools Grant Services** and the
**Rebuild My School Africa** regional intervention program.

This form helps identify special needs schools that may require grants, accessibility
support, assistive technology, learning materials, renovation, teacher development,
infrastructure improvement, safeguarding support, or other education intervention
services.

The form also allows individuals, companies, CSR partners, donors, diaspora supporters,
volunteers and education tourists to indicate interest in supporting, donating to, or
attending the regional project commissioning through **EduAid-Africa EduTourism 2027**.

Submitting this form does not guarantee school selection, grant approval, donation
allocation, regional voting outcome, or intervention approval. All nominations and
support interests are subject to eligibility review, evidence review, verification,
governance review, regional planning and NESA-Africa integrity approval.

## 5. Donation integrity notice (paste inside section 9)

Donation, pledge, sponsorship, public support, media visibility, endorsement,
EduTourism participation, or regional interest **does not guarantee** that any school
will be selected as a beneficiary.

All school selections must pass eligibility review, evidence review, regional
verification, governance approval, intervention planning and NESA-Africa integrity
review.

**No donor, sponsor or supporter may buy school selection.**

Donations and pledges are used to support approved education intervention planning,
subject to NESA-Africa / EduAid-Africa / Rebuild My School Africa governance, reporting
and implementation procedures.

## 6. Payment readiness rule (§7 of brief)

Do **not** activate live payment inside the Google Form unless payment collection,
receipt tracking, reconciliation, fraud control, donor records, acknowledgement emails
and admin reporting are ready.

If payment is not ready, use these labels only: *Donation Interest*, *Pledge Support*,
*Request Donation Follow-Up*, *Contact Me About Donation*. Never show fake payment
confirmation. Never claim a donation has been received.
